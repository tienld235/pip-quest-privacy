Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Pip Quest is a single-player offline game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (current run, unlocked dice and charms, star shards, language and sound settings) is saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. We never receive a copy.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID and technical information about your device. This is handled by Google, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Children

This app is rated for players 13 and older and is not directed at children.
